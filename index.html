MORE THAN A RUN

Movement with
meaning.

Run4Sudan is a community-led initiative bringing people together to raise awareness and support humanitarian relief in Sudan.

What started as an idea to turn solidarity into action has grown into a gathering of runners, families, volunteers, local businesses and supporters. Every step is a reminder of what we can do when we move together.
